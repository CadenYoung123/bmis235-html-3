Rome, Italy

Rome is one of the most historic cities in the world. Landmarks like the Colosseum, the Pantheon, and Vatican City sit within a short distance of each other, so you can walk through thousands of years of history in one day.

I want to see Rome for the architecture and the food, from fresh pasta to gelato on every corner.
